About my research

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UT Austin. My current focus is biomolecular modelling and protein engineering, working with Prof. Adam Klivans and Dr. Danny Diaz. I am interested in using machine learning to study and engineer proteins and other molecules.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UT Austin in May 2026, where I worked with Prof. Qiang Liu on generative modelling, flow-based methods, and multimodal learning. My work also spans multimodal robustness, benchmark design, and efficient generation.

  • Biomolecular modelling and protein engineering: My current research focus, including APEX: Anchored Protein Engineering, accepted to NeurIPS 2026.
  • Generative modelling and flow methods: Gumbel Distillation for Parallel Text Generation (ICLR 2026), Momentum Guidance (ECCV 2026)
  • Multimodal reliability and evaluation: Do Images Speak Louder than Words? (EACL 2026), Efficient and Long-tailed Generalization for Pre-trained Vision-Language Model (ACM KDD 2024)

Before UT Austin, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Nanjing University, where I worked with Prof. Yu-Feng Li. I have also worked on VLM reliability with Prof. Raymond Mooney.
